Design a comprehensive **Settings Page** for a CRM platform built for cleaning companies. The settings page should be context-aware, dynamic based on the user’s **role** (owner, manager, staff), and intuitive. It will cover both **personal settings** (e.g. password, profile) and **organization-level settings** (e.g. inviting team members, managing roles, company info).

# Objective:
Build a unified Settings interface where users can manage their own account, and (based on their role) manage their organization's information and team access.

---

# 🧑‍💼 Personal Settings (Visible to all users)

## ➤ Profile Info
- Name
- Email (read-only or editable depending on implementation)
- Role (e.g., "Manager", "Staff" — read-only)
- Organization name (read-only)
- Avatar or profile photo (optional)

## ➤ Change Password
- Current password
- New password
- Confirm new password
- Submit button + validation

## ➤ Notification Preferences *(optional)*
- Toggle job assignment email/SMS alerts
- Toggle lead notifications
- Toggle system updates

---

# 🏢 Organization Settings (Visible to Owner and Manager roles)

## ➤ Company Info
- Company Name
- Slug / Subdomain (if used)
- Timezone
- Business Hours (e.g., Mon–Fri 9am–5pm)
- Default Services (list of services offered like "Deep Clean", "Carpet Wash")
- Save button

## ➤ Team Management
### Member List
- Table of all team members with:
  - Name
  - Email
  - Role
  - Status (Active, Invited)
  - "Remove" or "Change Role" options (only visible to owner/manager)

### Invite New Team Member
- Email input field(s) for invite
- Role selector (`Staff`, `Manager`)
- Send Invite button

### Change Role (Only visible to Owners)
- Dropdown to promote/demote users
- Cannot change own role or demote other Owners

### Remove Team Member
- "Remove" button with confirmation prompt
- Optional checkbox to reassign leads/jobs owned by removed user

## ➤ Role Permissions Overview *(optional but recommended)*
- Read-only table describing what each role can do
  - View jobs, manage jobs, assign jobs, manage leads, invite users, manage settings

---

# 💳 Billing & Subscription *(Optional, if you're planning to charge businesses)*

## ➤ Subscription Plan
- Current plan
- Number of active team members
- Jobs/month limit
- “Upgrade Plan” button (links to Stripe or billing UI)

## ➤ Payment Method
- Card on file (masked)
- Expiry date
- Replace card

## ➤ Invoices
- List of past invoices
- Download PDF option

---

# 🔐 Security (Optional advanced settings)
- List of logged-in devices/sessions
- Option to log out from all devices
- 2FA toggle (if implemented)
- Audit log (if Owner/Manager): Shows recent critical actions (e.g. "John removed a team member", "Job reassigned")

---

# ✨ General UX Requirements
- Responsive design
- Tabbed interface or sidebar nav (`Personal`, `Organization`, `Billing`, `Security`)
- Sticky save buttons with real-time validation
- Role-based conditional rendering (hide org/team settings for `staff`)
- Clean visual hierarchy, ideally using cards or sections
